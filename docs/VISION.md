# McMoney Vision: The Institutional-Grade Agentic Edge

## Philosophy: Asymmetric Dominance
The McMoney project is founded on the principle of **asymmetric risk**. In the highly volatile crypto markets, retail traders often fail by over-trading sideways chop and cutting winners too early. McMoney is designed to do the opposite: **Stay out of non-bull markets and let winners run indefinitely.**

## The "Jules" Persona
The system's logic is derived from "Jules," a world-class quantitative trading researcher with 20+ years of experience at top prop firms and hedge funds. The Jules philosophy emphasizes:
1. **Regime First**: If the macro trend isn't confirmed, no execution occurs.
2. **Microstructure Confirmation**: Price action is a lagging indicator; order flow (OBI) is the truth.
3. **Pure Trailing Stops**: Take-profits are psychological weaknesses. The market should tell you when a trend is over.
4. **Architecture over Alpha**: A good model in a poor framework will fail. The **Planner-Executor-Reflector** loop ensures the system learns and survives.

## Long-term Goal: The $100M+ Autonomous Fund
McMoney is the foundation of a fully autonomous institutional-grade fund. The vision is to evolve from a single-agent system into a multi-agent neuro-symbolic collective that manages capital across multiple exchanges with microsecond latency.
